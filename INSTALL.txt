1. github.com > sign in > New repository (any name) > Create.
2. Click "uploading an existing file", drag in EVERYTHING from this folder
   (including the hidden .github folder), then Commit.
3. Open the Actions tab > "Build Angelead VST3" > wait ~5-10 min for the green check.
4. Open the finished run > Artifacts > download Angelead-VST3.
5. Unzip it and copy Angelead.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
6. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.
